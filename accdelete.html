What happens when you delete your account:

  • Permanent Account Removal: Your username, recovery email, hashed credentials, and authentication tokens are purged from our database[cite: 4, 7].
  • Gameplay & Ledger Data: Your lifetime balances, bilateral debt settlement records, side bets, and game histories are permanently deleted[cite: 4, 7].
  • Voice & Media: No audio or voice recordings are retained on our servers; temporary LiveKit sessions are torn down automatically upon disconnection.
  • Processing Timeline: Automated requests are processed within 24 to 48 hours. Guest sessions automatically expire without personal identifiers[cite: 4, 8].
