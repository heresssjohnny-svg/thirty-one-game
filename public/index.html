📖 How to Play 31 ✕

🎯 Objective

Collect a 3-card hand of the same suit totaling as close to 31 points as possible, or get 3-of-a-kind.

🔢 Card Values

• Aces: 11 points
• Face Cards (K, Q, J) & 10: 10 points
• Cards 2-9: Face value
• 3 of a Kind (e.g. 7-7-7): Worth 30.5 points in any suits!

👑 Initial Dealer Selection & New Players

• In a brand-new match, everyone cuts a face-down card. The lowest card deals!
• The winner of the game deals the new game.
• New players get dealt last.

🔄 Turn Play

On your turn, draw 1 card from the deck or discard pile, then choose 1 card from your 4 cards to discard.

🔔 Knocking

If you believe your hand is strong (at least 21 pts in 3+ player games, or 25 pts in heads-up), you can Knock before drawing. Everyone else gets one final turn before the showdown. Lowest hand loses 1 life!

⚡ The 21-out-of-31 Rule

If you make 31 by picking an Ace or Face card from the discard pile, and the same player fed you both the Ace and Face card, that player loses BOTH lives immediately! If you hit 31 from the deck, everyone else loses 1 life.

👁️ Spectator Hand Peeking

Spectators can request permission to view an active player's hand. If granted, they can watch that hand. If the spectator changes views or leaves, permission is immediately revoked and they must ask again!
⚠️ High Card in Discard!
There is an Ace/Face card on top of the discard pile.

Knocking counts as offering this card to the next player. If they take it and make 31, you could lose both lives!
