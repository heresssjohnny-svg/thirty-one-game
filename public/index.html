31! Game Rules & Mechanics

Object: Collect 31 points in a hand or avoid being the lowest scorer/losing all lives.

Card Values: Ace = 11 points. 10 through King = 10 points. 3 of a kind = 30.5 points. Points are calculated by cards of the same suit in your hand.

Instant Win: If any player hits 31 points at any time, the round stops immediately and all other players lose a life.

The 21 out of 31 Rule: If a player feeds another player an Ace and a face/10-card via the discard pile, and the next player picks it up, the feeding player loses all their lives!

Knocking: With 3+ players, knock requirement is 21+. In heads-up, knock requirement is 25+. The lowest score loses a life.
