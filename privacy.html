Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

Welcome to 31! ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any minimal information required to run our real-time multiplayer card game. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our web application and mobile app wrappers.

1. Information We Collect

Our game is designed with privacy in mind. We do not require account registrations, passwords, or personal tracking identifiers. The only data processed includes:

  • Usernames: The display name you choose locally when entering a game lobby or table.
  • Gameplay Data: Real-time actions during matches (such as card draws, wagers, room codes, and chat messages) transmitted over WebSockets strictly to facilitate multiplayer gameplay.
  • Voice Chat (Optional): If you toggle on the peer-to-peer Voice Chat feature, audio streams are routed directly between participants via WebRTC and are never recorded or stored on our servers.

2. How We Use Information

Any data processed is used exclusively for:

  • Connecting you to active game lobbies and maintaining real-time multiplayer synchronization.
  • Displaying your chosen username and game status to other players in your active lobby.

3. Data Storage and Security

We do not store permanent user profiles or personal records on external databases. Local preferences (such as audio volume or your saved display name) are stored securely on your own device using browser Local Storage.

4. Third-Party Services

Our game is hosted on cloud infrastructure provided by Render. Render may collect standard server logs (such as IP addresses and connection timestamps) automatically for security, uptime monitoring, and infrastructure maintenance.

5. Children’s Privacy

Our game does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. The game contains no targeted advertising or external tracking beacons.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted directly to this page with a revised effective date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via our project repository or support channels.
