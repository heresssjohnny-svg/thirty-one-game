How to Play 31!

🎯 The Objective

Be the last player standing with lives remaining! Each player starts with 2 lives by default. At the end of each round, the player(s) with the lowest hand score lose a life. Reach 0 lives and you're eliminated.

🃏 Hand Scoring

Only cards of the same suit are added together towards your score (Clubs, Diamonds, Hearts, or Spades):

  • Aces: 11 points
  • Face Cards (K, Q, J): 10 points each
  • Number Cards (2–10): Face value
  • Triplets (Special): 3 cards of the exact same rank (e.g. 7♠ 7♥ 7♦) are worth 30.5 points across any suits!

🔄 Gameplay Flow

On your turn, you must perform two actions in order:

  1. Draw 1 Card: Take either the top card from the deck OR the top face-up card from the discard pile (holding 4 cards total).
  2. Discard 1 Card: Tap 1 of your 4 cards to discard back to the center (leaving you with exactly 3 cards).

🔔 Knocking

  • You may only knock before picking up a card on your turn (with exactly 3 cards in hand).
  • Minimum Score to Knock: You must have at least 21 points (in 3+ player games) or 25 points (in 2-player games).
  • Every other player gets one final turn to draw and discard before hands are revealed.

⚡ The Blitz (Instant Win)

Holding 31 points (Ace + two 10-point cards in the same suit) triggers an immediate Blitz win! All other active players instantly lose a life.
