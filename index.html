How to Play 31!

🎯 Objective: Be the last player standing with lives remaining. Lowest hand score loses a life at showdown.

🃏 Hand Scoring: Only cards of the same suit count together:

  • Aces: 11 points
  • Face Cards (K, Q, J): 10 points
  • Number Cards: Face value
  • Triplets (3 of a kind): 30.5 points across any suits

🔄 Turn Flow: Draw 1 card (from Deck or Discard), then Discard 1 card from your 4-card hand.

🔔 Knocking: You can only knock with exactly 3 cards before drawing, if you meet the threshold (21+ for 3+ players, 25+ in heads-up). Every other player gets 1 final turn.

⚡ Blitz 31: Hitting 31 ends the round immediately, stripping a life from all opponents (or the player who fed the Ace and 10 of that suit under the 21 out of 31 rule).
