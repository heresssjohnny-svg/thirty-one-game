Rules of 31

• Collect 3 cards of the same suit. Highest point sum wins.

• Aces = 11, Face cards = 10, Number cards = face value.

• Three of a kind (triplets) = 30.5 points.

• 31 Points = Instant round win (all others lose 1 life).

• Knock before you draw if your hand is 21+ points (25+ in heads-up). You cannot knock after drawing a 4th card.
