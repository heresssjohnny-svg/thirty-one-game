📖 Complete 31! Tutorial & Rules

1. Objective & Hand Scoring

Collect the highest point total in a single suit with your 3 cards:

  • Aces: 11 points
  • Face Cards (K, Q, J): 10 points
  • Pip Cards (2–10): Face value
  • Three of a Kind (Triplets): Any 3 cards of the identical rank (e.g., 7♠ 7♦ 7♥) score exactly 30.5 points, beating any non-blitz 3-card combination!
2. Opening Dealer Selection

At table start, all players draw a face-down card from the deck pool. The lowest card deals (2 is lowest, Ace is highest). If players tie on the lowest card, a re-draw occurs. The dealer is marked with the [D] badge.

3. Gameplay & Turn Flow

Each player is dealt 3 cards. The dealer flips 1 card to start the discard pile. On your turn:

  • Draw 1 card from either the face-down DECK or the face-up DISCARD pile (holding 4 cards).
  • Discard 1 card back onto the pile to return to 3 cards.
  • Put-Back Discard Rule: If you pick the discard card, change your mind, and place that exact card right back down, your turn does not end—you must draw from the deck instead!
4. Knocking & The 21 / 25 Threshold

When confident in your hand, you can Knock on your turn before drawing:

  • You must complete at least 1 full orbit around the table before knocking is unlocked.
  • 3+ Players: Must hold at least 21 points to knock.
  • Heads-Up (2 Players): Must hold at least 25 points to knock.
  • Knocking triggers 1 final turn for every other player. Then all cards are revealed!
5. Round Resolution & Tie-Breakers

The player with the lowest score loses 1 life (starts with 3 lives):

  • Heads-Up Tie: If both players tie for lowest score in a 2-player game, no one loses a life!
  • 3+ Player Tie-Breaker: Tied players enter the live Tie-Breaker Draw stream. The player who draws the lowest card loses a life. If cards tie, players re-draw!
6. Blitz (31 Points Auto-Win)

If any player hits an exact 31 points (e.g., A♠ K♠ 10♠), the round ends immediately, and every other player loses a life!

7. Trackers & Side Bets

Top-Left Tracker: Shows who took the dealer's initial card. Top-Right Tracker: Reminds you if the next player took your discard. Tap any player's seat to propose $5 or $10 elimination side bets!
