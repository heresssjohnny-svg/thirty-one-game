How to Play 31!

🎯 Objective

Collect 31 points in a single suit, or have the highest hand score before someone knocks.

🃏 Card Scoring

  • Aces: 11 points
  • Face Cards (K, Q, J): 10 points
  • Number Cards: Face value (2-10)
  • Triplets (Three of a Kind): 30.5 points

🔔 Knocking Rule

You can only knock on your turn before drawing, while holding exactly 3 cards. Requires 21+ points (25+ heads-up).
