How to Play 31!

Objective: Collect 31 points in a single suit or achieve a higher hand score than your opponents before the round ends.

Scoring:

  • Numbered cards (2-10) are worth face value.
  • Face cards (Jack, Queen, King) are worth 10 points.
  • Aces are worth 11 points.
  • Three matching cards of the same rank (e.g., three 8s) score 30.5 points.

Gameplay Loop & Amended 21 out of 31 Rule:

  • Each player starts with 3 cards and 2 or more lives.
  • On your turn, draw 1 card from either the Deck or the Discard Pile, then discard 1 card from your hand.
  • If you hit exactly 31 points, the round ends immediately, and all opponents lose a life!
  • Amended 21 out of 31 Rule: If a player feeds an opponent an Ace and a 10-value card from the discard pile, the feeder only loses all lives if that fed player achieves a full 31 points at any point during the round!

Knocking: Once every player has taken at least 1 turn, if you are satisfied with your score (25+ in standard play, or 21+ in 3+ player games), you can click Knock. Every other player gets 1 final turn before hands are revealed. The lowest score loses a life!

Side Betting & Review:

  • 3+ Players: Tap any player's name to propose a First to Lose elimination bet.
  • Heads-Up: Tap any name for a Global Side Bet. When players accept via the right sidebar, review their acceptance to either Confirm or Decline!
